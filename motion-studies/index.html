Armada Motion Studies

Three resident app screens built from the Figma frames’ own layers and assets. Option A animates the screen in place. Option B opens tight on one section, pulls back to the whole app, then brings the rest in. Option C, built with the stageshow method, has a finger use the screen: every motion has a touch as its cause. All loop; Replay restarts one.

01 · Home

Home dashboard

Figma 10121:4626 · Dashboard, Multi Family
Option AIn place
  • MacroHeader drops, cards cascade 40ms apart, 12px rise, nav slides up last.
  • MicroBell rings once the cascade lands; 72° counts up from 64°.
Option BZoom reveal
  • MacroOpens on the thermostat card, only the 72° readout showing, scaled to fill the screen.
  • MacroPulls back to the full home in 1.1s; the remaining cards enter around it, nearest first.
  • MicroCount-up and bell ring run during the hold and after the reveal.
Option CTouch walkthrough
  • MicroA finger dismisses the event banner; the cards below close the gap in 450ms.
  • MacroDrag to scroll down the home and back, at the finger’s speed.
